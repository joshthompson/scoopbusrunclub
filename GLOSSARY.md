# Scoop Bus

Scoop Bus Run Club's website and its games. The games turn the club's runners into characters chased by the Scoop Bus.

## Language

### Run game

**Scoop Bus**:
The bus that chases runners along a Course. It picks up (scoops) anyone it catches, at any height: its **Scoop Arm** reaches up to a Platform or down below the Ground to grab them. In a Cave it has a **Drill** on its front in place of its scoop, and bores its way through the rock, too big for the Cave. In The Sewers it puts its Drill on and **Digs** its own way down through the street after whoever's gone down the manhole, and back up again after the Giant Vent. On The Cliff it drives off the cliff after you and floats across the Sea, and up onto the island.
_Avoid_: Sweeper, chaser

**Scooped**:
Caught by the Scoop Bus, which takes a runner out of the race.
_Avoid_: Eliminated, dead, out

**Pacer**:
A computer-controlled runner sharing the course with the Players.
_Avoid_: Bot, AI, NPC, the pack (in prose)

**Character**:
The runner a Player races as: a club member or a custom runner. Each Character can be used by at most one Player per Room.
_Avoid_: Avatar, skin, runner (for the choice itself)

**Course**:
One 5 km route, with its own hills, look, and Obstacles, Boosts and Rides, laid out the same every time. Courses come in a fixed order, roughly easiest first.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, map, track (Kristineberg's course is a running track)

**parkrun Course**:
A Course that is a real parkrun, such as Haga or Huddinge, with that parkrun's own hills. It's raced as "_name_ parkrun". Every other Course, Kristineberg included, goes by its name alone.

**Endless**:
Single-player play that goes round every Course in order, back to the first after the last.

**Run**:
One go at Endless, from the first Course until the runner is Scooped, with the score carried from Course to Course.
_Avoid_: Game, attempt; calling a Practice a Run

**Practice**:
One go at a single Unlocked Course on its own, outside Endless.

**High Score Table**:
The best scores for Endless, or for one Course (its points in one go, in a Run or in Practice). Each is kept in this browser, and online, where everyone's best goes on it, one each.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard (that's club results), Standings (that's a Room's)

**High Score Name**:
The name a player's scores go under on the online High Score Tables, and the Character whose face goes by it. Chosen the first time they send a score, and kept in this browser.

**Ticket**:
What the backend hands each Run and each Practice as it starts. A score is only taken onto an online High Score Table with its Ticket, and only if it adds up for the time it's taken.
_Avoid_: Signature, signing (nothing's signed: whatever signed it would be in the player's browser to copy)

**Unlocked**:
Said of a Course that can be Practised on this device: the first always, and any other once the Course before it has been finished here, in a Run or in Practice. Unlocking has nothing to do with Rounds.

**Ground**:
The lowest surface along a Course, following its hills, which runners stand on unless they're on a Platform. It can have Gaps.
_Avoid_: Floor, terrain (in prose), level

**Wall**:
The side of higher Ground, such as the next rooftop along or the far side of a Gap. A runner who runs into one at a run bounces back and stumbles (walked into, it just stops them), and drops down whatever is below: into a Gap, they are Fallen. Platforms have no Walls: runners pass through them from below.

**Ledge**:
A Wall that only stops a runner, never throws them back: each step of the Great Wall's staircase. They jump up it.

**Gap**:
A stretch of a Course with no Ground and no Platform underneath: going down one leaves a runner Fallen.
_Avoid_: Pit, hole (potholes are Obstacles you can trip in and get out of)

**Platform**:
A raised surface a runner can land on above the Ground, such as a ledge, a rooftop or a crane girder. Runners jump up through one from below, and drop off it by running off the end or ducking.
_Avoid_: Level, layer, floor

**Moving Platform**:
A Platform that moves, such as a girder swinging from a crane.

**Cave**:
A stretch of a Course under a hill, with rock over the Ground, or dug down under the sand, as through the Sphinx on the Pyramids. Some Platforms in a Cave are shelves of solid rock: there's no jumping up through those from below. From outside, a Cave is solid rock, only its way in and its way out showing; it opens up once someone racing goes in. On Scoop Cruises the ferry is a Cave: its plated side is the rock, its rooms open up once someone's aboard, and its sun deck is open to the sky.

**Ceiling**:
The underside of the rock over the Ground in a Cave, or of a solid rock shelf. A runner who jumps into it bumps their head and comes back down.
_Avoid_: Roof (a roof is Ground, on City Rooftop)

**Tunnel**:
Where the Ceiling comes down too low to stand under. A runner has to duck into it, and stays down until they're out, going through no slower than they went in. Run into one standing up, and it's a Wall.
_Avoid_: Tube, pipe

**Route**:
One of the ways through part of a Course where it divides, such as over a Cave's rock shelf or along under it, or along the top of Scoop Cruises' ferry.
_Avoid_: Branch (a branch is an Obstacle to duck under), path (the path is what everyone runs along)

**Fallen**:
Gone down a Gap, which takes a runner out of the race just as being Scooped does.
_Avoid_: Dead, lost

**Out**:
No longer in the race, either Scooped or Fallen.

**Obstacle**:
Something on a Course that a runner has to jump or duck, or else stumble: a rock, a branch, mud, a hurdle, a pothole, children playing football, geese on the path, a furby, a Zombie, a Swamp Man, or a Sewage Drain; or, out over the Sea, gulls, a hot-air balloon, a Storm Cloud, a wind turbine or a whale's spout, to fly round.
_Avoid_: Hazard

**Mummy**:
A bandaged mummy in the Sphinx's tomb on the Pyramids, shuffling to and fro, arms out: an Obstacle. The Scoop Bus Scoops any it reaches.
_Avoid_: Zombie (Zombies walk Halloween Town)

**Zombie**:
A club member risen from the grave, now and then just a skeleton, walking the wrong way along Halloween Town: an Obstacle. One that walks into an open grave is gone, and the Scoop Bus Scoops any it reaches. (Skeletons also run the right way, as Pacers.)
_Avoid_: Pacer (Pacers race the right way)

**Steam Vent**:
A grate in the Ground that puffs steam now and then, firing whoever's on it as it puffs high into the air. Not an Obstacle: nothing to stumble on. A **Giant Vent** is The Sewers' way out: it fires whoever's on it right up out of the sewer and on, back onto the street.

**Bouncy Toadstool**:
Furby Fields' Steam Vents: great spotted toadstools that squash flat as someone steps on one, and spring them high into the air, onto a candyfloss cloud.
_Avoid_: Mushroom, trampoline

**Giant Furby**:
A furby as big as a hill, on the path in Furby Fields: a Cave, its head. Its eye follows whoever's coming, and its beak swings open as they near, its mouth the way in; inside, its workings, and out past its tail at the back.

**Swamp Man**:
A creature in a pool of sludge in The Sewers, who now and then rises out of it and grabs whoever's in reach, holding them still till he sinks back: an Obstacle. Jump him.
_Avoid_: Zombie, Mummy

**Sewage Drain**:
A grate in the Ceiling under the street in The Sewers that, in waves, pours out what's washed down off the street. Anyone under it while it's pouring is splattered, and stumbles: an Obstacle, to time going through.
_Avoid_: Chute, waterfall

**Boost**:
Something on a Course that speeds a runner up: fika (on Machu Picchu, a can of Inca Cola), a Cheer, a Pumpkin, a Barcode Token, or a Ring.
_Avoid_: Pickup, power-up, Obstacle

**Pumpkin**:
A Boost that sits on a runner's head and keeps them faster until they next stumble. A runner wears at most one.

**Barcode Token**:
A parkrun barcode, floating in the run-in just before every parkrun Course's finish: the last Boost, and the biggest. Run through it and it's scanned.
_Avoid_: Barcode (on its own: a real parkrunner's barcode is theirs, not a thing on a Course)

**Ride**:
Something that carries a runner along for a while: a corgi, in Gamla Stan, or on Machu Picchu a llama, at the Pyramids a camel (now and then, one race in ten, a two-humped Bactrian camel among them), in Huddinge's forest a deer, in Furby Fields a unicorn, or on The Cliff a Glider; or flying over, a goose, or on the Great Wall a dragon. One on the ground leaps when its rider jumps, with them on its back; ducking gets them off it.
_Avoid_: Mount, vehicle

**Glider**:
The Cliff's Ride: a hang glider, parked along the top of the cliff, more of them than a Room has Players. Run into one and you're off on it, lying along under it, out over the Sea, steering it up and down, faster or slower, for good: it only comes down once it's over the island. Down into the Sea on one, you crash, and you're Fallen. Pacers take the spare ones; those that don't get one go off the cliff.
_Avoid_: Kite, plane

**Sea**:
The Cliff's Gap, from the foot of its cliff out to the island. Down into it is Fallen, there and then.
_Avoid_: Water, ocean

**Ring**:
A Boost in the sky over the Sea: glide through one for a burst of speed. There for everyone, once each.
_Avoid_: Hoop

**Storm Cloud**:
A dark cloud over the Sea that now and then flickers, then strikes lightning down into it: an Obstacle, to fly over, or to time going under.
_Avoid_: Thundercloud

**Cheerer**:
A club member standing by a Course who gives a Cheer, a Boost, to whoever runs past.
_Avoid_: Volunteer (that's someone who really volunteered at a parkrun)

**Run Director**:
The club member who counts everyone down at the start of a Course, as at a real parkrun.

**Collectable**:
Anything a runner can have on a Course: each fika, each Ride, a Pumpkin, a Barcode Token. Its first time collected is kept on this device, and how many times since.

**Tourist**:
Someone stood in the street in Gamla Stan, in everyday clothes, never kit: an Obstacle to jump, in groups.

### Multiplayer

**Room**:
One multiplayer session that up to 8 Players join, identified by its Room Code.
_Avoid_: Lobby, game, server, session

**Room Code**:
The short code that identifies a Room and lets anyone join it, typed in or scanned as a QR code.
_Avoid_: Room ID, PIN

**Host**:
The device that owns a Room, runs the one true race and always shows it. It may have no Players of its own.
_Avoid_: Server, leader, screen

**Player**:
A person racing in a Room: a Local Player, a Phone Controller's player or a Remote Player.
_Avoid_: User, peer, client

**Local Player**:
A Player on the Host's own screen, using half of its keyboard or one of its gamepads.
_Avoid_: Couch player

**Phone Controller**:
A phone in the same room as the Host that acts only as one Player's controller and never shows the race.
_Avoid_: Pad, remote, controller app

**Remote Player**:
A Player on another device, usually over the internet, that shows the race itself and has its own controls. Joined from a computer, it shows the whole Room just as the Host does, but for how to join; from a phone, the race goes above its buttons.
_Avoid_: Online player, guest (guests are club visitors), client

**Starter**:
The first Player in a Room who's still in it, however they're playing. They choose how the Room goes and start it, once everyone else who's picked is ready: on their phone, or at the Host if they're a Local Player. A Room never starts by itself. If the Starter drops out or leaves, the next Player along takes over.
_Avoid_: Host (that's the device), leader, captain, admin

**parkrun**, **Half Marathon**, **Marathon**, **Backyard Ultra** (of a Room):
How far a Room's Starter has it go, chosen in its lobby. Its Courses are always chosen at random and shuffled: five of them; ten; every Course once; or every Course, round and round, until the Starter (or whoever's at the Host) Finishes it between Rounds. Each ends on the Podium.
_Avoid_: Playlist, mode (in prose), Endless (that's single-player)

**Round**:
One Course raced by a Room's Players. It ends when only one Player hasn't been Scooped. A Room's Rounds go through its Courses in a shuffled order.
_Avoid_: Race, Run (that's single-player Endless), heat, match

**Standings**:
The points each Player has built up across a Room's Rounds, one point per Player outlasted. Level on points, whoever did better in the last Round is ahead. They start again from nothing each time the Room goes back to its lobby.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard (that's club results), scoreboard, high scores

**Podium**:
The end of a Room's go, after its last Round: the top three in the Standings on their steps, with gold, silver and bronze medals, and everyone else under them. It goes back to the lobby by itself, or when the Starter says.
_Avoid_: Winners' screen, final results

**Room List**:
The public list of open Rooms that Hosts have chosen to list.
_Avoid_: Lobby, server browser, matchmaking
