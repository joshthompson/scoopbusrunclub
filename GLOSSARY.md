# Scoop Bus

Scoop Bus Run Club's website and its games. The games turn the club's runners into characters chased by the Scoop Bus.

## Language

### Run game

**Scoop Bus**:
The bus that chases runners along a Course. It picks up (scoops) anyone it catches, at any height: its **Scoop Arm** reaches up to a Platform or down below the Ground to grab them. In a Cave it has a **Drill** on its front in place of its scoop, and bores its way through the rock, too big for the Cave.
_Avoid_: Sweeper, chaser

**Scooped**:
Caught by the Scoop Bus, which takes a runner out of the race.
_Avoid_: Eliminated, dead, out

**Pacer**:
A computer-controlled runner sharing the course with the Players.
_Avoid_: Bot, AI, NPC, the pack (in prose)

**Character**:
The runner a Player races as: a club member or a custom runner. Each Character can be used by at most one Player per Room.
_Avoid_: Avatar, skin, runner (for the choice itself)

**Course**:
One 5 km route, with its own hills, look, and Obstacles, Boosts and Rides, laid out the same every time. Courses come in a fixed order, roughly easiest first.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, map, track (Kristineberg's course is a running track)

**parkrun Course**:
A Course that is a real parkrun, such as Haga or Huddinge, with that parkrun's own hills. It's raced as "_name_ parkrun". Every other Course, Kristineberg included, goes by its name alone.

**Endless**:
Single-player play that goes round every Course in order, back to the first after the last.

**Run**:
One go at Endless, from the first Course until the runner is Scooped, with the score carried from Course to Course.
_Avoid_: Game, attempt; calling a Practice a Run

**Practice**:
One go at a single Unlocked Course on its own, outside Endless.

**Unlocked**:
Said of a Course that can be Practised on this device: the first always, and any other once the Course before it has been finished here, in a Run or in Practice. Unlocking has nothing to do with Rounds.

**Ground**:
The lowest surface along a Course, following its hills, which runners stand on unless they're on a Platform. It can have Gaps.
_Avoid_: Floor, terrain (in prose), level

**Wall**:
The side of higher Ground, such as the next rooftop along or the far side of a Gap. A runner who runs into one at a run bounces back and stumbles (walked into, it just stops them), and drops down whatever is below: into a Gap, they are Fallen. Platforms have no Walls: runners pass through them from below.

**Gap**:
A stretch of a Course with no Ground and no Platform underneath: going down one leaves a runner Fallen.
_Avoid_: Pit, hole (potholes are Obstacles you can trip in and get out of)

**Platform**:
A raised surface a runner can land on above the Ground, such as a ledge, a rooftop or a crane girder. Runners jump up through one from below, and drop off it by running off the end or ducking.
_Avoid_: Level, layer, floor

**Moving Platform**:
A Platform that moves, such as a girder swinging from a crane.

**Cave**:
A stretch of a Course under a hill, with rock over the Ground. Some Platforms in a Cave are shelves of solid rock: there's no jumping up through those from below.

**Ceiling**:
The underside of the rock over the Ground in a Cave, or of a solid rock shelf. A runner who jumps into it bumps their head and comes back down.
_Avoid_: Roof (a roof is Ground, on City Rooftop)

**Tunnel**:
Where the Ceiling comes down too low to stand under. A runner has to duck into it, and stays down until they're out, going through no slower than they went in. Run into one standing up, and it's a Wall.
_Avoid_: Tube, pipe

**Route**:
One of the ways through part of a Course where it divides, such as over a Cave's rock shelf or along under it.
_Avoid_: Branch (a branch is an Obstacle to duck under), path (the path is what everyone runs along)

**Fallen**:
Gone down a Gap, which takes a runner out of the race just as being Scooped does.
_Avoid_: Dead, lost

**Out**:
No longer in the race, either Scooped or Fallen.

**Obstacle**:
Something on a Course that a runner has to jump or duck, or else stumble: a rock, a branch, mud, a hurdle, a pothole, children playing football, geese on the path, a furby, or a Zombie.
_Avoid_: Hazard

**Zombie**:
A club member risen from the grave, now and then just a skeleton, walking the wrong way along Halloween Town: an Obstacle. One that walks into an open grave is gone, and the Scoop Bus Scoops any it reaches. (Skeletons also run the right way, as Pacers.)
_Avoid_: Pacer (Pacers race the right way)

**Steam Vent**:
A grate in the Ground that puffs steam now and then, firing whoever's on it as it puffs high into the air. Not an Obstacle: nothing to stumble on.

**Boost**:
Something on a Course that speeds a runner up: fika (on Machu Picchu, a can of Inca Cola), a Cheer, a Pumpkin, or a Barcode Token.
_Avoid_: Pickup, power-up, Obstacle

**Pumpkin**:
A Boost that sits on a runner's head and keeps them faster until they next stumble. A runner wears at most one.

**Barcode Token**:
A parkrun barcode, floating in the run-in just before every parkrun Course's finish: the last Boost, and the biggest. Run through it and it's scanned.
_Avoid_: Barcode (on its own: a real parkrunner's barcode is theirs, not a thing on a Course)

**Ride**:
Something that carries a runner along for a while: a corgi, or on Machu Picchu a llama; or flying over, a goose, or on the Great Wall a dragon.
_Avoid_: Mount, vehicle

**Cheerer**:
A club member standing by a Course who gives a Cheer, a Boost, to whoever runs past.
_Avoid_: Volunteer (that's someone who really volunteered at a parkrun)

**Run Director**:
The club member who counts everyone down at the start of a Course, as at a real parkrun.

**Collectable**:
Anything a runner can have on a Course: each fika, each Ride, a Pumpkin, a Barcode Token. Its first time collected is kept on this device, and how many times since.

**Tourist**:
Someone stood in the street in Gamla Stan, in everyday clothes, never kit: an Obstacle to jump, in groups.

### Multiplayer

**Room**:
One multiplayer session that up to 8 Players join, identified by its Room Code.
_Avoid_: Lobby, game, server, session

**Room Code**:
The short code that identifies a Room and lets anyone join it, typed in or scanned as a QR code.
_Avoid_: Room ID, PIN

**Host**:
The device that owns a Room, runs the one true race and always shows it. It may have no Players of its own.
_Avoid_: Server, leader, screen

**Player**:
A person racing in a Room: a Local Player, a Phone Controller's player or a Remote Player.
_Avoid_: User, peer, client

**Local Player**:
A Player on the Host's own screen, using half of its keyboard or one of its gamepads.
_Avoid_: Couch player

**Phone Controller**:
A phone in the same room as the Host that acts only as one Player's controller and never shows the race.
_Avoid_: Pad, remote, controller app

**Remote Player**:
A Player on another device, usually over the internet, that shows the race itself and has its own controls.
_Avoid_: Online player, guest (guests are club visitors), client

**Endless**, **Marathon**, **Half Marathon** (of a Room):
How the Host has a Room go, chosen in its lobby: every Course, shuffled, round and round for ever; every Course once, shuffled; or five of them, at random, shuffled.
_Avoid_: Playlist, mode (in prose)

**Round**:
One Course raced by a Room's Players. It ends when only one Player hasn't been Scooped. A Room's Rounds go through every Course in a shuffled order.
_Avoid_: Race, Run (that's single-player Endless), heat, match

**Standings**:
The points each Player has built up across a Room's Rounds, one point per Player outlasted.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard (that's club results), scoreboard, high scores

**Room List**:
The public list of open Rooms that Hosts have chosen to list.
_Avoid_: Lobby, server browser, matchmaking
